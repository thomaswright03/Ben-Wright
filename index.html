About Ben

Player profile

Ben has been competing since he was a young kid. He was U.S. Kids Golf Salt Lake City Player of the Year three times and qualified for the U.S. Kids World Championship at Pinehurst in 2018, 2019 and 2020. He has won 40 local tournaments since 2018.

At East High he has been the team's top seed and had the lowest scoring average in the region as both a freshman and a sophomore, earning two varsity letters. He also played shooting guard on the basketball team, is a Team Titleist member, and volunteers with his church's Young Men's service projects.
